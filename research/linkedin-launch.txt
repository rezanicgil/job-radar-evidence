If a job asks for production experience with Docker, does “Dockerized a personal API and ran it locally” meet the requirement?

I built Job Radar Evidence to help applicants inspect questions like this before they apply. Paste a Turkish or English job requirement, open a CV, and the demo surfaces candidate passages with page references. You decide which requirements the CV supports. The public demo processes your CV in your browser.

I published the layers of a multilingual classifier trained on 69 fictional requirement–CV pairs. On 30 new synthetic cases grounded in ten employers’ requirements it found none of the ten partial matches. That result shaped the live demo: applicants see the CV evidence and confirm what it supports.

You can also paste an entire English posting and select the skill sentences to review. That selector achieved 75.0% F1 on 2,404 deduplicated SkillSpan test sentences. The Türkiye market view shows technology mentions across 69 readable postings from the November 9 snapshot, with links to the sources.

Try the fictional example first. If you work in mobile, embedded systems or another software field, what requirement could look convincing on a CV while still missing a crucial detail? A made-up passage and the phrase that changes your judgment would help shape the next evaluation set.
