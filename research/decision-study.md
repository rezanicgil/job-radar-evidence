# Does a pre-application brief help?

**Question.** Can a person identify their next action on a saved posting faster while preserving opportunities they would consider?

## Before the session

Ask the participant to name a recent application decision, the information they needed and where they looked for it. Record their constraints in their own words. If a different step consumed most of their time, record that observation before testing the brief.

Select ten unfamiliar postings. Balance language, length and difficulty across two sets of five. Draft an English brief for each posting and link each claim to a verbatim passage. Mark every brief as human-authored. Keep posting text available in both conditions. Use public links or participant-supplied excerpts for the session.

## During the session

Randomize the order. Show five postings in their original form and the other five with the brief. Each participant sees each posting once. For a second participant, reverse which set gets the brief. Start timing when the posting opens and stop when the participant chooses **review**, **skip** or **ask the recruiter**. Record the reason. Avoid coaching.

## After the session

Review each skip against the full posting and the participant's stated constraints. Record whether the participant changes their decision and why. Inspect unsupported claims individually. Ask which single line changed an action, and which line they would remove.

Report median decision time per condition, the number of reversed skips and the actual cases. With one participant or unmatched posting difficulty, treat results as discovery observations. Use verified recurring errors to design original Turkish and English training examples. Preserve separate evaluation cases.
